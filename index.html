Jamie Uploader

Jamie Uploader is a private, single-user tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel @jamiereeveshq. It uploads the owner's own short videos to that channel using the YouTube Data API.

It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, no other users, and it never accesses anyone else's account or data.
