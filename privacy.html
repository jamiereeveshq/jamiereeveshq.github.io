Privacy Policy

Jamie Uploader · Effective 17 September 2026

Jamie Uploader is a private, single-user tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel @jamiereeveshq (the "operator"). It uploads the operator's own videos to the operator's own channel. It has no other users and collects no data about anyone else.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

Jamie Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. What information is processed

  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens Google issues when the operator authorises the app.
  • The video file, title, description and privacy setting the operator chooses to publish.
  • The video ID and upload status YouTube returns, to confirm the upload succeeded.

No names, contact details, location, device or advertising identifiers, analytics or viewer data are collected.

3. How it is used

Only to upload the operator's own videos to the operator's own channel and report the result. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored only on one private device controlled by the operator. Video files are deleted after upload. No data is sold or shared with anyone; it is sent only to YouTube to perform the upload.

5. Retention and revoking access

Tokens are kept while the integration is in use. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions, which immediately stops the app from uploading. Stored tokens are then deleted.

6. Changes

Any changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.
